AI search guide

How to get your business recommended by ChatGPT, Google AI and the others

Let AI search tools read your website, keep your Google Business Profile complete, collect reviews, and answer your customers' questions in plain words on your own website. Then track which questions AI recommends you for, and work on the gaps every month.

Updated November 23, 2026, by Vic and Jen Westcott, who run First Local Club and Oak Bay Clean.

1. Let AI search tools read your website

ChatGPT finds websites for its search answers with a crawler called OAI-SearchBot. OpenAI says sites that block it will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers. Google asks you to keep crawling allowed in your robots.txt file and by your hosting company.

Check your robots.txt file at yourwebsite.com/robots.txt. If it blocks OAI-SearchBot or Googlebot, ask whoever built your site to change it.

2. Make sure Google can index your pages

Google says there are no extra requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. Your page has to be indexed and eligible to show in Google Search with a snippet. You can check both for free in Google Search Console.

3. Fill in your Google Business Profile

Google says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and prominence, and that businesses with complete and accurate info are more likely to show up. Its AI features page also asks you to keep your Business Profile up to date. Add your categories, services, hours and service area, and update them when they change.

4. Get reviews, and reply to them

Google says more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking, and that helpful replies help you stand out. At Oak Bay Clean we ask for the review the same day as the clean.

5. Answer your customers' questions on your website

Google asks for important content to be in text and written for people. Put your prices or price ranges, the areas you serve, what's included and how to book on your own pages, in plain words.

6. Track it every month

Check the same questions every month so you can see what changed. We track 22 questions for Oak Bay Clean across ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini, Perplexity and Bing Copilot. In the week of November 17, 2026, Oak Bay Clean was mentioned in 70% of answers, first of the 10 Victoria cleaning companies we track. The next was at 32%.
