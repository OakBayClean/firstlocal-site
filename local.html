Your seat in the club

We let in one business of each kind per area. Your area is up to 5 zip codes (or the first 3 characters of Canadian postal codes). While you're a member, the seat is yours and nobody else of your kind gets in.

  • Want more areas? Each extra area is its own seat.
  • Your seat is yours for as long as you stay. If you leave, it goes to the next business on the waitlist.
  • Seat taken? We'll put you on the waitlist for your area.
