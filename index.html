Questions people ask

What is First Local Club?

First Local Club is a members-only club for local businesses, run by Elgin Road Productions Ltd. in Victoria, BC. Every month we work to get members recommended first when people ask ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini and Perplexity for their kind of business nearby. We let in one business of each kind per area.

How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT and Google AI?

Let AI search tools read your website, keep your Google Business Profile complete, collect reviews, and answer your customers' questions in plain words on your own website. Then track which questions AI recommends you for every month. Our step-by-step guide has each step, with sources from Google and OpenAI.

Who can join?

Cleaning businesses, and local businesses like massage clinics, studios, restaurants and shops. Each area has one seat for each kind of business.

What counts as an area?

Up to 5 zip codes in the US, or up to 5 postal code areas in Canada (the first 3 characters of a postal code). Each extra area is its own seat.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your kind of business. Prices are on the cleaning businesses page and the local businesses page. Every membership is month to month.

How do I join?

Book a free 30-minute review. We check your area is open and go through your AI search report with you. If it's a fit, you claim your seat through our secure Stripe checkout.
