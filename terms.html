2. The service

Each month we:

  • track how often AI search tools (such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini and Perplexity) mention your business, across 15 search questions agreed with you

  • audit up to 50 pages of your website

Setup covers choosing your search questions and competitors, your first page audit and your first report.

We use third-party software and AI tools to deliver the service. A person on our team reviews every article before it goes to you.

  • Your seat ends when your plan is cancelled or stays unpaid. We may then offer it to the next business on the waitlist for that area.

Setup also covers your marketing files: an ideal customer persona, main offers, brand voice and business blueprint. For local businesses outside cleaning we build these with you. For cleaning companies we start from the Cleaning Company Blueprint versions and fill in your details.
